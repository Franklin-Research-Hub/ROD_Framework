GOVERNANCE RULES THAT APPLY

From Metro Nashville General Records Schedule:
  - Administrative correspondence: retain 2 years
  - Contracts: retain 7 years after completion
  - Activity reports: retain 3 years
  - Official records: subject to Public Records Commission oversight
  - Archival records: permanent retention

From Public Records Act (TPRA):
  - All state, county, and municipal records are presumed open for inspection
  - Citizens have the right to access public records during business hours
  - Government records are public unless exempt by law
  - Records custodians must provide records timely and efficiently

From Accessibility Requirements:
  - Government websites must comply with ADA
  - Digital content must meet WCAG standards
  - Public records must be accessible in multiple formats
  - Elected officials' published content must meet accessibility standards
