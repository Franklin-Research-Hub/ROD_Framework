This streamlined approval path is for framework proposals submitted by the creator that have been assessed as having no identified threats.

PURPOSE

To enable rapid approval of low-risk creator proposals without requiring full departmental approval chains.

Metro IT review is not automatically required for this path. The decision to involve Metro IT remains at Terrell Wilson's discretion.

QUALIFYING CRITERIA

[ ] Proposer is Victoria Love Franklin
[ ] Proposal does not violate public records policy
[ ] Proposal does not handle sensitive or restricted data
[ ] Proposal does not require department operational sign-off
[ ] Proposal does not require department governance approval
[ ] Proposal is categorized as LOW THREAT or NO THREAT
[ ] Terrell Wilson agrees that Metro IT review is not required at this stage

If any criterion is not met, the proposal must use the FULL CHAIN approval process.

_______________________________________________________________________________

APPROVAL PROCESS

Step 1: Proposer Submits
• Victoria submits proposal
• Includes brief description and statement that no threats were identified
• Submits to Terrell Wilson

Step 2: Terrell Reviews
• Terrell reviews proposal for Metro IT relevance
• Terrell decides whether Metro IT review is necessary
• If no Metro IT review is needed, proposal moves forward under this path
• If Metro IT review is needed, proposal may be routed elsewhere

Step 3: Completion
• If APPROVED: Proposal may proceed to implementation
• If APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS: Conditions must be documented
• If REJECTED: Proposer revises and resubmits

Timeline: 2-3 business days typical
