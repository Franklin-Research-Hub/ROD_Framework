TERRELL WILSON DISCRETION NOTE

Metro IT Review Authority & Decision Framework

Authority Statement:
Terrell Wilson (Deputy Register Information Technology) holds final decision-making authority over whether Metro IT review is required for framework proposals under this approval system.

Metro IT review is not automatically required for every proposal.
Metro IT review is triggered only at Terrell Wilson's discretion based on the risks, compliance needs, or technical concerns present in each proposal.

_______________________________________________________________________________

DISCRETION AUTHORITY

Terrell determines review is required when a proposal involves:
• Public records handling or retention
• Accessibility compliance
• Website or public-facing digital content
• Sensitive or restricted data
• IT security or privacy risks
• System integration or platform configuration
• Technical validation or architectural review
• Unclear policy or governance concerns

Terrell may decide review is not required when:
• Proposal is limited to framework documentation
• Proposal involves no data handling or records retention
• Proposal has no accessibility or compliance implications
• Proposal is internal governance or organizational only
• Proposal presents no IT risk

_______________________________________________________________________________

DISCRETION PROCESS

1. Terrell reviews the proposal and threat assessment.
2. Terrell asks whether the proposal creates a Metro IT policy, technical, security, or compliance concern.
3. If YES: Terrell determines required review level and may escalate.
4. If NO: Terrell may approve under the creator-only or streamlined path.
5. Terrell documents the discretion decision on the approval form.
