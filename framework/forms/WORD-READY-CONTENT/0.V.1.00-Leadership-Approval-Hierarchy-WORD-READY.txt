APPROVAL HIERARCHY STRUCTURE

This document establishes the formal leadership approval chain for all framework proposals, governance decisions, and Metro IT compliance validations.

_______________________________________________________________________________

RANK 1: JACK BROWN
Chief Deputy Register

Authority: Operational approval and initial sign-off

Responsibilities:
• Reviews framework proposals
• Assesses operational impact and disruption
• Identifies operational risks and concerns
• Provides initial operational sign-off
• Routes approved proposals to Karen Johnson
• Returns rejected proposals to proposer with explanation

Approval Options:
☐ APPROVED - Proposal passes operational review
☐ APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS - Proposal approved pending conditions
☐ REJECTED - Proposal fails operational review

Timeline: 3-5 business days typical

_______________________________________________________________________________

RANK 2: KAREN JOHNSON
Register of Deeds

Authority: Department-level governance and compliance approval

Responsibilities:
• Reviews Jack Brown's operational assessment
• Assesses governance and compliance implications
• Identifies conflicts with Metro Nashville policy
• Provides department-level approval
• Escalates to Terrell Wilson if IT issues identified
• Routes approved proposals to Terrell Wilson for final review
• Returns rejected proposals to proposer with explanation

Approval Options:
☐ APPROVED - Proposal passes governance review
☐ APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS - Proposal approved pending conditions
☐ REJECTED - Proposal fails governance review
☐ ESCALATED - Proposal requires additional review beyond this chain

Timeline: 3-5 business days typical

_______________________________________________________________________________

RANK 3: TERRELL WILSON
Deputy Register Information Technology

Authority: Final IT governance and Metro IT compliance approval

Responsibilities:
• Reviews Jack Brown's operational assessment
• Reviews Karen Johnson's governance assessment
• Conducts final IT governance and Metro IT compliance review
• Validates against public records policy
• Validates against accessibility requirements (ADA, WCAG)
• Validates against security and data handling standards
• Determines whether Metro IT review is required
• Provides final IT authority sign-off
• Returns rejected proposals to proposer with explanation

Approval Options:
☐ APPROVED - Proposal passes all reviews
☐ APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS - Proposal approved pending conditions
☐ REJECTED - Proposal fails compliance review
☐ ESCALATED - Proposal requires higher-level review

Metro IT Review Determination:
☐ Metro IT review is NOT required - Proceed to implementation
☐ Metro IT review IS required - Route to appropriate technical review
☐ Metro IT review is CONDITIONAL - Conditions identified

Timeline: 3-5 business days typical

_______________________________________________________________________________

COMPLETE APPROVAL CHAIN

Sequential Flow:
Proposal Submitted → Jack Brown → Karen Johnson → Terrell Wilson → Final Decision

All Three Approvals Required: YES

Total Timeline: 5-10 business days typical

Approval Completed When: All three approvers have signed and dated the approval form

_______________________________________________________________________________

DECISION OUTCOMES

APPROVED
Framework proposal has successfully passed all three levels of review.
• Proposal may proceed to next implementation layer
• Complete documentation retained in framework archive
• Implementation timeline begins immediately upon approval

APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS
Framework proposal has been approved with specific conditions or remediation required.
• Conditions must be documented and agreed to by proposer
• Proposer must confirm conditions are met before implementation
• Terrell Wilson must verify conditions have been addressed
• Implementation may begin after condition verification

REJECTED
Framework proposal has failed at one or more approval levels.
• Proposer receives rejection explanation
• Proposer must address concerns and resubmit
• Resubmitted proposal goes through full approval chain again
• Original rejected form retained in archive

ESCALATED
Framework proposal requires additional stakeholders or higher-level review.
• Escalation reason and path documented
• Additional reviewers identified
• Escalation timeline established
• All approvers and proposer notified of escalation status
