TERRELL WILSON SIGNOFF PROTOCOL

This protocol defines how a framework proposal or validation item is formally reviewed and signed off by Terrell Wilson.

SIGNOFF REQUIREMENTS

All validation nodes must document:
• the authority reviewed
• the relevant requirement or rule
• the validation decision
• Terrell's approval or rejection
• date of decision
• final action taken

DECISION RULES

• If a validation passes, the proposal may proceed under the appropriate approval path.
• If a validation fails, the proposal must be revised and resubmitted.
• If a validation is conditional, conditions must be documented and approved before the proposal proceeds.
• If the issue is unclear, the proposal remains pending until the decision is resolved.
