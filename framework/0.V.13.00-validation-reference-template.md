This node validates that the framework aligns with Metro Nashville public records rules and records governance standards.

VALIDATION SUMMARY

The framework is reviewed against Metro Nashville's official public record standards and records management policies.

Assessment: The framework aligns with the broader government requirements that:

- public records are open unless exempt by law
- government information must be retained under public records schedules
- government content is subject to records management procedures
- official digital content should be treated as part of the government records lifecycle

This validation does not establish a standalone Metro IT website publishing policy, but it confirms that the documents and framework are subject to Metro's official records and transparency obligations.

VALIDATION RESULT: PASSED ✓

Framework governance aligns with Metro Nashville public records requirements.
