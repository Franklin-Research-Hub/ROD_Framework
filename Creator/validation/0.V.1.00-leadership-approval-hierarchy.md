------------------------------------------------------------

This node establishes the formal leadership approval hierarchy for framework governance, validation, and Metro IT compliance decisions.

APPROVAL PROCESS

1. JACK BROWN (Chief Deputy Register)
   - Reviews framework proposal
   - Assesses operational impact
   - Provides initial operational sign-off
   - Routes to Karen Johnson upon approval

2. KAREN JOHNSON (Register of Deeds)
   - Reviews Jack's assessment
   - Provides department-level approval
   - Escalates to Terrell Wilson if IT issues identified
   - Routes to Terrell Wilson for final IT compliance review

3. TERRELL WILSON (Deputy Register Information Technology)
   - Final IT governance and Metro IT compliance review
   - Validates against public records policy
   - Validates against accessibility requirements
   - Validates against security/data handling
   - Provides final IT authority sign-off

All three approvals required for framework implementation.

DECISION OUTCOMES

- APPROVED: Framework may proceed to implementation
- APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS: Framework approved pending remediation
- REJECTED: Framework denied; requires redesign and resubmission
- ESCALATED: Requires additional stakeholder review beyond approval chain
