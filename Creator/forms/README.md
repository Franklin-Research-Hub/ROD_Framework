## How to Use

1. **Copy the appropriate text source into Word** for your proposal type and save your own `.docx` or PDF
2. **Fill out all required fields** with your proposal information
3. **Follow the approval routing** indicated in the form
4. **Obtain signatures** from the required approvers
5. **Save completed form** with descriptive filename including date
6. **Archive** the completed form with your proposal documentation

## Conversion to PDF

Locally created Word documents can be converted to PDF for:
- Digital signing (using DocuSign, Adobe Sign, etc.)
- Email workflow
- Archival and record-keeping
- Printing for physical signature

## Fillable Form Fields

The text sources include blanks and checkbox notation, not interactive controls. Add fillable fields in your local Word or PDF editor if required; no interactive downloads are supplied here.
