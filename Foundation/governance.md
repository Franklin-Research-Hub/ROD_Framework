# Governance

## 1. Purpose
This document applies controlled change and stewardship rules to the working Foundation baseline.

## 2. Scope
Foundation owns structural, metadata, and validation guidance. Changes affecting other branches or root documents must also preserve the [master tree](../MASTER-TREE-BRANCH.md) and [contribution rules](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## 3. Principles
- Preserve branch boundaries and the Creator → Foundation dependency.
- Keep record content in its owning branch, not in governance guidance.
- Distinguish proposed changes from the adopted working baseline.
- Preserve ownership and licensing restrictions.

## 4. Change Control
1. Identify the proposed change, purpose, affected documents, and dependency impact.
2. Obtain approval from Victoria Love Franklin or an explicitly documented delegate before adopting the change.
3. Revise affected definitions, links, local trees, and indexes together.
4. Update master structure and TOC together if repository-wide navigation changes.
5. Check [validation criteria](validation-references.md) and retain a reviewable change history.

## 5. Stewardship
Victoria Love Franklin retains ownership and approval authority. Delegated maintenance does not transfer ownership or automatically authorize changes to authority, licensing, or layer structure. No delegation is implied by a document role or metadata field.

## 6. Status
Active-working governance baseline. Revisions to these rules require the same controlled approval process.
