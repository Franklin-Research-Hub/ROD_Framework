# Contributing

## 1. Scope and Permission
`Franklin-Research-Hub/ROD_Framework` is a governed, proprietary framework archive, not an open public contribution platform. Contributions require authorization from Victoria Love Franklin and remain subject to [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).

## 2. Current Structure
Follow [MASTER-TREE-BRANCH.md](MASTER-TREE-BRANCH.md):
- Root files provide repository-wide entry, navigation, definitions, and legal guidance.
- `Foundation/` (Layer 1) owns structural, governance, metadata, and validation rules.
- `Creator/` (Layer 2) owns creator records and their application, registered through [Creator/INDEX.md](Creator/INDEX.md).

Do not use older folder layouts to classify new work. Creator depends on Foundation, not the reverse.

## 3. Documentation Rules
- Keep section numbering local and consecutive from 1 within each document.
- Do not interpret section numbers as node identifiers or `0.00` as a version.
- Preserve branch ownership, canonical record attribution, and the distinction between entry, index, tree, application, and record documents.
- Keep the TOC aligned with the master tree; do not surface branch-only records there.
- Use active-working or baseline language for architecture still under development.
- Do not change ownership, authority, or license terms without explicit owner approval.

## 4. Change Workflow
1. Confirm permission and identify the owning branch and affected documents.
2. Propose changes under [Foundation governance](Foundation/governance.md).
3. Update the smallest complete set of files, including affected indexes, local trees, and links.
4. Update master structure and TOC together when structural navigation changes.
5. Check [validation criteria](Foundation/validation-references.md), metadata meaning, Markdown, and internal links.
6. Submit changes for review; a proposal does not become an approved baseline merely because it exists.

## 5. Approval and Status
Victoria Love Franklin retains approval authority. Contact the owner directly for contribution permissions and licensing inquiries. This workflow is an active-working baseline and may be revised through controlled approval.
